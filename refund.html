Refund and Replacement Policy

Report an issue

For damaged, missing, spoiled or incorrect products, please contact support within 6 hours of delivery with your order ID and a description. Photos are helpful where available. This reporting window helps us investigate fresh-product complaints and does not extinguish statutory consumer rights.

Resolution

We will review the complaint and offer an appropriate replacement, refund or other remedy where warranted. Refund method and expected timing will be communicated after review. No return of unsafe food is required unless safe handling arrangements are agreed.

Cancellation

Contact support before dispatch to request cancellation. If an order is cancelled by us, any amount paid for unfulfilled items will be refunded as applicable.
