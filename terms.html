1About this website

This website is a catalogue. It is not an online shop. You cannot buy, pay or check out on this website. Orders are made only through a conversation with us on WhatsApp.

2How ordering works

  • Tapping "Order on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send.
  • Sending a message is a request for price and availability. It is not a confirmed order.
  • An order is confirmed only when we reply and confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

3Prices

Prices of fresh produce change with the market, season and quality, so we do not display prices on this website.

We share the current price with you on WhatsApp before your order is confirmed, and you are free to accept or decline it.

4Availability

Fresh produce is a natural product and stock varies through the day. An item shown on the website may sometimes be unavailable. We will tell you on WhatsApp if your selection cannot be fulfilled, and may suggest an alternative.

5Product photos and descriptions

Photos are for illustration. The actual size, shape and colour of fruits and vegetables can differ slightly because they are natural products.

6Quantity, delivery and payment

Quantities, delivery area, delivery time, delivery charges (if any) and the payment method are agreed with you on WhatsApp when your order is confirmed. Our current service area is listed in the website footer and on the Help & Support page.

7Quality and replacements

We check our produce for quality. If something you receive is not fresh or not as agreed, message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible, ideally with a photo, and we will work with you to put it right.
