Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

GreenBasket is a fresh fruits and vegetables catalogue. You browse our products on this website and place your order by messaging us on WhatsApp. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is involved and how we treat it.

1. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, log in, or share a password.
  • We do not take online payments or card details on this website.
  • We do not run a checkout or store orders on this website.
  • We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

2. When you order on WhatsApp

When you tap an "Order on WhatsApp" button, your device opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (for example, the product and quantity you selected). The message is only sent when you press send in WhatsApp.

Once you send it, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, your name as shown on WhatsApp, and the content of your message. We use this only to reply to you, confirm today's price and availability, and arrange your order and delivery.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and your use of it is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

3. Information handled by third-party services

  • Google Fonts: This website loads fonts from Google. Your browser may share technical details such as your IP address with Google when it does so.
  • Website hosting: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, it may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and browser type) for security and operation.
  • Product data: Product details shown on the website may be managed in a Google Sheet. It contains product information only, not customer information.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not set our own cookies to track you. Third-party services listed above may use standard technical mechanisms to deliver their content.

5. How we use and protect your information

We use the details you share on WhatsApp only to serve your order and respond to your questions. We do not sell your personal information. We share it with others only where needed to complete your delivery, or where the law requires.

6. Your choices

You can ask us on WhatsApp to stop messaging you or to delete our chat and the details we hold about you, and we will act on reasonable requests. You can also block or delete the chat from your own device at any time.

7. Children

This website is intended for adults placing grocery orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Message us on WhatsApp and we will be happy to help.
