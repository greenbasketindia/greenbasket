How to order

You do not need an account, password or app. Ordering takes about a minute.

1

Explore

Browse the products on our website. Tap + on items to build your list.

2

Order on WhatsApp

Tap Order on WhatsApp. Your list is already typed in the message.

3

Confirm

We reply with today's price and availability, and confirm your order and delivery.

How WhatsApp ordering works

  • Tapping an order button opens WhatsApp on your phone (or WhatsApp Web on a computer) with a message ready for GreenBasket.
  • Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. You can edit the message first, for example to change the quantity.
  • Your order is confirmed only after we reply and agree the details with you on WhatsApp.

Product availability

Fresh produce is seasonal and stock can change during the day. If an item you want is not available, we will tell you on WhatsApp and, where possible, suggest an alternative. Items marked "Ask on WhatsApp" on the website need a quick check with us first.

Price information

Prices of fruits and vegetables move with the market, so we do not display prices on the website. We share today's price with you on WhatsApp before you confirm anything.

You are never charged just for sending a message. You decide whether to go ahead once you have seen the price.

Service area

Not sure if we deliver to you?

Send us your locality on WhatsApp and we will confirm whether we can deliver there.

Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login, signup or password. Just send us a message on WhatsApp.

Can I pay on the website?

No. The website is a catalogue only. Payment and delivery details are agreed with you on WhatsApp when your order is confirmed.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will update it if the order has not been packed yet.

What if something I receive is not fresh?

Message us on WhatsApp with a photo and we will work with you to put it right.
