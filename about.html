About Us

About GreenBasket

GreenBasket is a local fresh fruits and vegetables service for Hanumangarh. We make it simple to get good produce: you choose what you need, send your list on WhatsApp, and we take care of the rest.

Who we are

GreenBasket is built around one simple idea: buying fresh produce should be easy and honest. There is no app to install and no account to create. You browse our catalogue, build your list, and talk to us directly on WhatsApp.

Fresh produce focus

Fruits, vegetables, leafy greens, herbs and seasonal produce, listed clearly with the quantity options that suit each product.

Simple ordering

Choose a quantity, add it to the cart, and send the whole list in one WhatsApp message. Everything happens in one conversation.

Local service

We serve Hanumangarh and nearby areas, and confirm delivery details with you before your order is final.

What we offer

Our catalogue covers everyday needs and the best of the season. Availability changes with the day and the season, and the website shows what we currently list.

How ordering works

Four simple steps, and no account needed.

1

Choose

Browse the products and pick the quantity you need for each one.

2

Add to cart

Build your list in the cart. Change a quantity or remove an item any time.

3

Send on WhatsApp

Tap one button. Your list is already typed in a message to GreenBasket.

4

Confirm

We reply with today's price and availability and confirm your order and delivery.

Prices of fresh produce change from day to day, so we do not show them on the website. We share the current price on WhatsApp and you decide whether to go ahead. See our price information.

Our commitments

These are the standards we hold ourselves to in every order.

Honest pricing

You see the current price on WhatsApp before anything is confirmed, with no obligation.

Quality focus

We check produce for quality. If something is not right, message us with a photo and we will work with you to put it right.

Clear communication

If an item is unavailable we tell you, and where possible suggest an alternative.

Your privacy

No account, no online payment and no ad trackers on this website. See our Privacy Policy.

Where we deliver

GreenBasket serves Hanumangarh and nearby areas. Delivery timing, charges and the payment method are agreed with you on WhatsApp when your order is confirmed.

Not sure whether we cover your area? Check the service area details or ask us on WhatsApp.
