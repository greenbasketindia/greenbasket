About Us

About GreenBasket

GreenBasket is a local service for fresh fruits and vegetables in Hanumangarh. We make it simple to get good produce: you pick what you need, message us on WhatsApp, and we take care of the rest.

What we do

We offer a catalogue of everyday fruits and vegetables, along with leafy greens, herbs and seasonal produce. You browse the products on our website and send your list to us on WhatsApp. We then confirm today's price and availability with you, and arrange delivery.

Freshness first

Fruits and vegetables are picked for your order, so what reaches you is as fresh as we can make it.

Quality focus

We sort and check produce before it is packed. If something is not right, you can tell us and we will work to fix it.

Personal service

No app and no sign-up. You talk to a real person on WhatsApp, the way you would with a trusted local shopkeeper.

How ordering works

Ordering is simple and does not need an account.

1

Explore

Browse fresh produce on our website and choose what you need.

2

Order on WhatsApp

Tap the button. Your list is already typed in a WhatsApp message to GreenBasket.

3

Confirm

We share today's price and availability, and confirm your order and delivery with you.

Prices of fresh produce change from day to day, so we do not show them on the website. We always confirm the price with you on WhatsApp before your order is final.

Local and customer-focused

GreenBasket is built around the needs of local families. We keep things honest and straightforward: clear communication, fair handling of every order, and quick help whenever you need it on WhatsApp.
